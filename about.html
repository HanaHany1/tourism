Our Story

Exceptional Healthcare.
Extraordinary Egypt.

Medora was founded in Cairo in 2016 by a team of Egyptian medical professionals, international patient coordinators, and travel specialists who saw a clear opportunity: Egypt's hospitals were world-class — but the world didn't know it yet.

Today, Medora has helped over 8,000 patients from 60+ countries receive life-changing medical treatments in Egypt — and discover its ancient wonders along the way.

🎯 Mission

To make Egypt the world's most trusted medical tourism destination — where exceptional care meets extraordinary culture.

🔭 Vision

A world where every patient can access world-class healthcare in Egypt — affordable, safe, and deeply memorable.

Our Values

What Drives Us

🛡️

Safety Above All

Every Medora partner hospital holds GAHAR accreditation — Egypt's national standard equivalent to JCI. We never compromise on patient safety.

💎

Genuine Quality

We personally inspect every partner hospital, verify outcomes data, and track patient satisfaction continuously — not just at sign-up.

🤝

Egyptian Warmth

🔍

Radical Transparency

Every price is all-inclusive and itemised. No hidden fees, no surprise extras, no hard sales — ever.

🌍

Global Access

We support patients in 15+ languages and work with partners in Europe, the Gulf, and Africa to ensure seamless access to care.

🏺

Pride in Egypt

We are Egyptian-founded and proudly promote our country's world-class medical talent, culture, and extraordinary heritage to the world.

Our Journey

Medora's Milestones

2016

Founded in Cairo

Started with 5 partner hospitals in Cairo and a team of 10. Focused on dental and cosmetic surgery for Gulf and European patients.

2018

Red Sea Expansion

Launched Hurghada and Sharm El Sheikh operations. 1,000-patient milestone reached. Partnership with Steigenberger and Four Seasons hotels sealed.

2020

GAHAR Audit Programme

Established Medora's exclusive GAHAR quality audit protocol — the first Egyptian medical tourism facilitator to publish its own public outcomes data.

2022

Upper Egypt Destinations

Added Luxor and Aswan packages featuring Nile cruise recovery programs. Integrated telemedicine aftercare portal for all Medora patients.

2024

WMTA Recognition

Listed in the World Medical Tourism Alliance's Top 100 facilitators. Reached 6,000 patients served. Opened European liaison offices in London & Düsseldorf.

2026

8,000+ Patients & 30+ Hospitals

Expanded to Fayoum and Ain Sokhna. Medora now covers 8 Egyptian cities, 30+ hospitals, and supports patients from 60+ countries.
