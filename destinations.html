Cairo

📍 Capital · Nile Delta

Egypt's capital is the medical hub of the Middle East. Cairo hosts the country's most advanced hospitals, internationally certified specialists, and robust international patient infrastructure. Combine treatment with the Pyramids, the Egyptian Museum, the Nile, and Old Cairo's bazaars.

Hurghada

📍 Red Sea Governorate

Hurghada is Medora's premier recovery destination — 5-star beach resorts minutes from cosmetic and dental clinics. The warm Red Sea climate is ideal for post-op healing. World-class diving and snorkelling for those cleared for activity.

Sharm El Sheikh

📍 South Sinai

Club-class 5-star resorts at the tip of the Sinai peninsula. Sharm International Hospital provides full cosmetic and orthopaedic services for international patients — with resort-to-clinic transfers arranged by Medora. Exceptional for wellness retreats and spa recovery.

Alexandria

📍 Mediterranean Coast

The "Pearl of the Mediterranean" offers a relaxed European-style coastal setting with Alexandria International Hospital — one of Egypt's top dental and fertility centres. Walk the Corniche, visit the Bibliotheca Alexandrina, and enjoy superb Mediterranean seafood during recovery.

Luxor

📍 Upper Egypt · Nile Valley

The world's greatest open-air museum. Luxor health retreats are ideal for post-dental or light post-op recovery — calm Nile-side hotels, hot air balloon flights at sunrise, and private guided tours of Karnak and the Valley of the Kings.

Aswan

📍 Upper Egypt · Nubia

Egypt's most tranquil destination — where Nile feluccas drift past granite temples and Nubian villages. Aswan Nile cruise packages allow patients to recover in pure relaxation. The dry warm climate is particularly therapeutic for respiratory and post-surgical recovery.

Fayoum

📍 Western Desert Oasis

Egypt's largest natural oasis — birdwatching at Wadi El-Rayan, desert hot springs, and the stunning Lake Qarun. Fayoum resorts are ideal for meditative post-op recovery and holistic wellness programs away from urban noise.

Ain Sokhna

📍 Red Sea · Near Cairo

Just 1.5 hours from Cairo International Airport, Ain Sokhna is Cairo patients' fast escape to the Red Sea — luxury private chalets, calm beaches, and excellent Arabic seafood. Ideal for a short post-procedure rest before flying home.
