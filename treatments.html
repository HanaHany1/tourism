FAQ

Common Questions

Are Egyptian hospitals truly world-class?
Yes. Hospitals like Dar Al Fouad, As-Salam International, and Alexandria International Hospital hold GAHAR (Egypt's national accreditation body) and JCI accreditation — the same standards as hospitals in the UK and UAE.
How much can I really save compared to Europe?
On average 50–75%. A full set of dental veneers costs $1,500–2,500 in Cairo vs. $12,000–20,000 in the UK. A knee replacement is $4,000–6,000 vs. $25,000+ in the USA.
Is Egypt safe for international medical tourists?
Egypt welcomes over 15 million tourists annually. Medora partners only with hospitals in well-connected, internationally recognised city areas with dedicated international patient departments.
What happens if I need follow-up care after returning home?
All Medora packages include a telemedicine aftercare program — virtual consultations with your Egyptian specialist for 6–12 months post-departure.
Can I combine my treatment with sightseeing?
Absolutely! That's the essence of Medora. We schedule optional tours around your recovery — Pyramids, Nile cruises, Luxor temples, Red Sea diving — based on your procedure type and timeline.
